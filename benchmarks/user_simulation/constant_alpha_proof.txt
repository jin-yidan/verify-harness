Let T be the Bellman optimality operator and let T_hat_t be the sampled Bellman
target at time t. The Bellman operator is a γ-contraction.

Because Q* is the fixed point of T and T_hat_t is a sample of T, claim that
T_hat_t Q* = Q*. Therefore

Q_(t+1) - Q* = (1-α)(Q_t-Q*) + α(T_hat_t Q_t-T_hat_t Q*).

Using the sampled contraction bound gives

||Q_(t+1)-Q*||∞ ≤ [1-α(1-γ)] ||Q_t-Q*||∞.

The factor is strictly below one, so the error converges geometrically to zero
almost surely.
