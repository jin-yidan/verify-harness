# Verification reporting

For mathematical verification runs, lead the final user-facing conclusion with
the mathematical status when it is established. In particular, when an exact
counterexample refutes the submitted theorem or claim, state plainly:
"The claimed theorem is false."

Put implementation or formal-coverage qualifications after that conclusion.
Do not use this wording when only a submitted proof step is refuted while the
theorem itself may still be true; in that case state that the proof is invalid
instead. Preserve the evidence level and any harness verdict without upgrading
it.
